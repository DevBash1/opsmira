# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
By taking part you agree to uphold it.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and not the person, and do not
harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this, and may ban people
who keep doing so.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form on this repository (choose "other"),
or message a maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. Reports are handled privately.
